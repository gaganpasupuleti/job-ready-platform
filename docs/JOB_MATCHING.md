# Job Matching (Build 10)

Deterministic **requirement coverage** — not hiring probability.

## Formula

- Required skills: **70%**
- Preferred skills: **15%**
- Target role alignment: **10%**
- Location/remote preference (if configured): **5%**

Skill readiness uses effective scores from practice evidence.

## Empty states

- No mapped job skills: "Not enough mapped requirements"
- No user evidence: "Complete practice to compare your evidence" (no 0% shown)
